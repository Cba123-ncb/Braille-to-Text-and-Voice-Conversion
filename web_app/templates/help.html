How to Use Braille Reader

Learn how to take perfect photos for accurate Braille recognition

Photography Guidelines

Key Requirements: Take photos in lighting that creates contrast between Braille dots and shadows. Light should come from the front or behind you, not from above.
Correct

Proper front lighting creates good contrast

Incorrect

Light from above, side, or behind creates poor contrast

Incorrect

Must photograph from directly above

Additional Tips:
  • Resolution: Minimum 1000 pixels in both directions (most phones meet this requirement)
  • Position: Photograph the page from directly above
  • Lighting: Avoid side lighting - use front or back lighting
